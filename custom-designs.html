FAQs

Custom questions, clearly answered.

A few practical details for your design brief.

Can I use a stencil if I’m new to kolam?

Yes. Start with a simple, open pattern on a clean, level surface. Use a small amount of fine powder and lift the stencil straight up. A little practice helps.

How do I choose a size?

Measure the available floor area and leave walking clearance around the pattern. 1 inch equals 2.54 cm. Confirm final dimensions before ordering.

How should wooden or MDF stencils be cleaned?

Gently brush off dry powder and store the stencil flat in a dry place. Avoid soaking wood or MDF, and check the maker’s instructions for your material.

Can a design be personalized?

Names, selected motifs and dimensions can be discussed through a custom enquiry. Not every fine line or script is suitable for cutting; artwork should be reviewed before confirmation.

Can I purchase directly on this website?

Browse designs and request a confirmed quote before ordering. Online checkout is currently unavailable.
